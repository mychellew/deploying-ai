City: Toronto
Attractions: CN Tower, Royal Ontario Museum, Distillery District
Best Time: May–September
Description: Toronto is the largest city in Canada and a major cultural and financial hub with diverse neighborhoods and vibrant city life.

City: Vancouver
Attractions: Stanley Park, Granville Island, Capilano Suspension Bridge
Best Time: June–September
Description: Vancouver is known for its stunning natural scenery, surrounded by mountains and ocean, making it ideal for outdoor activities.

City: Montreal
Attractions: Old Montreal, Mount Royal, Notre-Dame Basilica
Best Time: May–October
Description: Montreal blends European charm with modern culture, offering historic architecture, festivals, and a lively food scene.

City: Calgary
Attractions: Calgary Tower, Calgary Stampede, Banff National Park (nearby)
Best Time: June–August
Description: Calgary is known for its western culture and proximity to the Rocky Mountains, making it a gateway to outdoor adventures.

City: Ottawa
Attractions: Parliament Hill, Rideau Canal, National Gallery of Canada
Best Time: May–September
Description: Ottawa is Canada's capital city, rich in history and home to national museums and important landmarks.

City: Quebec City
Attractions: Château Frontenac, Old Quebec, Plains of Abraham
Best Time: June–September (summer) or December–February (winter festivals)
Description: Quebec City is famous for its historic European-style architecture and charming old town.

City: Edmonton
Attractions: West Edmonton Mall, Elk Island National Park, Art Gallery of Alberta
Best Time: June–August
Description: Edmonton is known for its festivals and one of the largest shopping malls in the world, along with a strong arts scene.

City: Winnipeg
Attractions: The Forks, Canadian Museum for Human Rights, Assiniboine Park
Best Time: May–September
Description: Winnipeg offers a mix of cultural attractions and historic sites with a strong arts and museum presence.

City: Halifax
Attractions: Halifax Waterfront, Peggy’s Cove, Maritime Museum of the Atlantic
Best Time: June–October
Description: Halifax is a coastal city known for its maritime history, scenic views, and fresh seafood.

City: Victoria
Attractions: Butchart Gardens, Inner Harbour, Royal BC Museum
Best Time: March–September
Description: Victoria, located on Vancouver Island, is known for its mild climate, gardens, and charming waterfront.

City: St. John’s
Attractions: Signal Hill, Quidi Vidi Village, Jellybean Row Houses
Best Time: June–August (for icebergs and whales)
Description: As North America's easternmost city, St. John's is famous for its colorful steep streets, rugged coastline, and a world-class music and pub scene on George Street.

City: Charlottetown
Attractions: Province House, Victoria Park, Anne of Green Gables sites (nearby)
Best Time: June–September
Description: The "Cradle of Confederation," this charming seaside capital features Victorian architecture, red-sand beaches nearby, and a deep-rooted history in Canadian politics.

City: Fredericton
Attractions: Beaverbrook Art Gallery, Officers' Square, Saint John River
Best Time: June–October
Description: New Brunswick’s capital is a riverside gem known for its vibrant craft beer scene, historic garrison district, and lush greenery.

City: Niagara Falls
Attractions: Horseshoe Falls, Clifton Hill, Niagara-on-the-Lake wineries
Best Time: June–August (peak weather) or September–October (wine harvest)
Description: A world-famous destination dominated by the powerful waterfalls, complemented by a kitschy entertainment district and a sophisticated nearby wine region.

City: Saskatoon
Attractions: Remai Modern Art Gallery, Meewasin Valley Trails, Wanuskewin Heritage Park
Best Time: June–August
Description: Known as the "City of Bridges," Saskatoon offers a surprisingly hip arts scene and beautiful prairie landscapes along the South Saskatchewan River.

City: Kelowna
Attractions: Okanagan Lake, Myra Canyon Trestles, Mission Hill Winery
Best Time: June–September (summer sun) or January–March (skiing)
Description: Located in the heart of wine country, Kelowna is a playground for fruit picking, water sports, and exploring over 40 nearby vineyards.

City: Whistler
Attractions: Whistler Blackcomb, Peak 2 Peak Gondola, Lost Lake
Best Time: December–March (skiing) or June–August (mountain biking/hiking)
Description: A world-class resort town that hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics, offering year-round alpine adventures and a lively pedestrian village.

City: Yellowknife
Attractions: Aurora Village (Northern Lights), Old Town, Great Slave Lake
Best Time: August–September or December–March (for Aurora Borealis)
Description: The capital of the Northwest Territories is one of the world's best places to view the Northern Lights, blending frontier history with modern Indigenous culture.

City: Whitehorse
Attractions: Miles Canyon, S.S. Klondike National Historic Site, Takhini Hot Pools
Best Time: June–August (Midnight Sun) or January–March (Aurora)
Description: Known as the "Wilderness City," Whitehorse serves as the gateway to the Yukon's vast landscapes and gold rush history.

City: Iqaluit
Attractions: Sylvia Grinnell Territorial Park, Nunatta Sunakkutaangit Museum, Frobisher Bay
Best Time: July–August (for mild weather and Arctic flowers)
Description: The capital of Nunavut offers a rare look at Inuit culture, Arctic wildlife, and the stark, beautiful landscapes of Canada’s newest territory.
